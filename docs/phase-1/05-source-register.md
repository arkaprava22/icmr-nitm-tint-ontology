## Source acceptance checklist

Before an evidence item can support a public claim, record:

- publisher and responsible organization;
- source type and tradition/system represented;
- stable identifier, edition, publication date, and access date;
- original language and any translation provenance;
- copyright, licence, permitted reuse, and attribution;
- document location and integrity information where legally retainable;
- the exact passage or structured record assessed by the reviewer;
- applicability, limitations, and conflicts identified during review.

## Important distinction

An authoritative publisher does not make every statement high-quality clinical
evidence. Source authority, evidence design, cultural provenance, and reviewer
assessment are separate fields in the model.
