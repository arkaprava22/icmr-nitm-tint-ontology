# Phase 1 Delivery Backlog

## Completed foundation

- Product vision, roles, MVP boundary, and success measures drafted
- Claim-centred ontology design selected
- Core RDF/OWL vocabulary created
- Initial SHACL validation rules created
- Contribution, review, provenance, and publication boundaries defined
- Safety and traditional-knowledge governance drafted
- Competency questions recorded

## Stakeholder workshops

| Workshop | Participants | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Product boundary | Sponsor, public users, product owner | Approved MVP journeys and exclusions |
| Clinical safety | Practitioners, reviewers, pharmacovigilance lead | Risk tiers and publication rules |
| Ontology | Domain experts, terminologists, data engineers | Approved classes, relations, mappings |
| Contribution governance | Practitioners, legal/privacy, editors | Identity, consent, conflict, review policy |
| Language and inclusion | Translators, accessibility users, regional experts | Launch languages and naming workflow |

## Discovery research

- Interview at least 8 public users across language and digital-literacy levels
- Interview at least 6 practitioners across the initial systems
- Observe 3 reviewers performing source and safety assessment
- Test 30 representative search questions and 10 misspelling/transliteration cases
- Inventory candidate government, classical, research, and terminology sources
- Record licence, update cycle, identifiers, format, and ingestion permission

## Ontology validation tasks

- Model 25 representative remedies and 40 use claims as a pilot dataset
- Include at least 10 multilingual/local-name ambiguity cases
- Include contraindication, interaction, vulnerable-population, and deprecation cases
- Run every competency question against the pilot graph
- Review the model with at least two independent domain experts
- Add workflow-aware SHACL rules after the editorial decisions are approved
- Publish namespace, versioning, deprecation, and change-management policies

## Phase 1 exit criteria

- Stakeholder decision log has owners and signed decisions
- All 12 competency questions work against the pilot dataset
- No published claim can pass validation without provenance and safety review
- Practitioner and reviewer journeys have tested low-fidelity prototypes
- Source inventory distinguishes reusable, restricted, and unknown-license material
- Architecture and delivery estimates for Phase 2 are accepted

## Recommended six-week schedule

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sponsor alignment, interviews, source inventory |
| 2 | Public search journeys, safety and governance workshop |
| 3 | Ontology workshop and first pilot records |
| 4 | Practitioner/reviewer prototype tests and ontology revision |
| 5 | Competency-query, SHACL, language, and privacy validation |
| 6 | Decision closure, Phase 1 sign-off, Phase 2 estimate |
